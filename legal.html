Legal & Security

Privacy, data handling, and the basics an enterprise vendor questionnaire asks first.

Plain-language, not a wall of boilerplate. This page covers how this site handles visitor data, how client code and credentials are handled during a project, and a downloadable summary for procurement review.

Privacy Policy

What happens with your information on this site.

What's collected

Standard web analytics (page views, referring source, approximate location from IP, device type) and anything you submit directly — your name, email, and project details via the contact form or a direct email.

What's not collected

No sale of visitor data to third parties, ever. No tracking across other sites for ad targeting. No data collected from the contact form is used for anything other than responding to your inquiry.

Cookies

This site uses only the minimum cookies/local storage needed for the site to function (such as remembering a closed announcement bar). No third-party advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can request a copy of, correction to, or deletion of any information you've submitted by emailing junaid.aideveloper@gmail.com directly — handled personally, not routed through a support queue.

Client Data & Code Handling

How access, code, and credentials are actually handled during a project.

The one-paragraph version every client — especially an enterprise-adjacent one — actually wants before signing. For the AI-specific side of this, see the AI Trust Center.

⚿

Access control

Project credentials (repo access, environment variables, API keys) are scoped to what's needed for the specific build, stored in environment-specific secrets managers — never committed to source control or shared over unencrypted channels.

◍

Offboarding

At project handover or engagement end, access is transferred or revoked as agreed — rotated keys, removed collaborator access, documented in writing so there's a clear record of what changed.

⬡

Code ownership

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the client owns all code, infrastructure, and deliverables produced during the engagement — no vendor lock-in, no held-hostage repositories.

◎

Confidentiality

Project details, client data, and business context are treated as confidential by default. A signed NDA is available on request before any scoping call that requires it.

For Procurement & Vendor Review

Security profile — the questions every vendor questionnaire asks.

A one-page summary answering the handful of questions that come up in almost every enterprise vendor review, so a procurement contact doesn't have to extract them from a sales call.

QuestionAnswer
Entity typeIndependent solo contractor / sole proprietor, not an incorporated agency
Data residencyConfigurable per project — Supabase/PostgreSQL supports region selection; discussed during scoping
EncryptionTLS/SSL in transit on all deployed applications; encryption at rest provided by infrastructure providers (Supabase, Vercel)

InsuranceAvailable on request depending on project scope and jurisdiction
