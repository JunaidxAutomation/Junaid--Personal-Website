AI Trust Center

How AI is actually used in what I build, in plain language.

AI automation is the core of what I sell — so a vague "we use AI responsibly" line isn't enough. This page states directly what's AI-generated, what's human-reviewed, how client data is handled around AI systems, and where the real limits are.

Governance Approach

Aligned to recognized frameworks — stated honestly about what that does and doesn't mean.

For a solo developer, formal certification (ISO/IEC 42001) isn't realistic or proportionate — that's an audited management-system standard built for organizations with dedicated compliance teams. What is both realistic and genuinely useful is structuring AI-related decisions the way the NIST AI Risk Management Framework describes: a voluntary, four-function structure for identifying and managing AI risk, not a checkbox.

G

Govern

Written internal policy on what AI is and isn't used for in client-facing systems, reviewed as each new project starts — not a one-time document that goes stale.

M

Map

Before any AI feature ships, I document what it touches — what data goes in, what decisions it influences, and what happens if its output is wrong.

Ms

Measure

AI outputs that affect a real decision (legal search results, qualification scoring) are tested against known-correct cases before and after any model or prompt change.

Mn

Manage

A defined path exists for what happens when an AI system gets something wrong — human review, correction, and a record of what changed and why.

ⓘ Stated precisely: this means AI-related work is structured in alignment with the NIST AI RMF's four functions. It is not a claim of NIST certification (the framework has no certification path) or ISO/IEC 42001 certification. If a client's vendor questionnaire specifically requires ISO 42001 certification, that should be raised directly during scoping — it is not something a solo independent developer can currently provide.
Where AI Is Used

What's AI-generated, and what's human-reviewed.

01

AI legal document search (Verity Legal)

Embedding-based search surfaces relevant laws and documents from an indexed database. The AI ranks and retrieves — it does not generate legal conclusions, and every surfaced result links back to its actual source document for verification, never a paraphrase presented as the source.

02

AI legal assistant personas (CaseMind, ContractBot, ResearchBot)

These generate drafts and suggestions grounded in retrieved source material. They are built and disclosed as drafting aids, not as a substitute for a licensed legal professional's review and sign-off — a distinction kept explicit in the product itself, not just in a footnote.

03

Lead qualification & outreach (LeadGen AI)

GPT-based scoring against a defined ICP and personalized first-touch message generation. Qualification criteria are explicit and client-set, not an opaque black-box score — a client can always see why a lead was scored the way it was.

04

General automation & workflow agents

Custom agents built per engagement follow the same rule: any step where an AI output could materially affect a business or legal outcome gets a defined human checkpoint, agreed during scoping — not assumed.

Data & Model Handling

Where your data goes when AI is in the loop.

QuestionAnswer
Which AI providers are usedAnthropic's Claude API and OpenAI's GPT API, called directly through their standard commercial APIs — no self-hosted or third-party model relay in between.
Is client data used to train modelsNo. API-tier usage of both providers is used under their standard commercial terms, which do not train on API inputs by default. This is confirmed per-project, not assumed.
Where is data storedPrimary data storage is Supabase/PostgreSQL with row-level security per tenant. Data residency requirements (e.g. EU-only storage) are a scoping question, not an afterthought.
Who can access client dataAs a solo developer, I am the only person with production access during development. Access is removed at handover unless an ongoing retainer is agreed — see the offboarding note below.
What happens at offboardingCredentials, API keys, and access are rotated or revoked at the end of an engagement. This is a checklist step at handover, not something that happens only if asked.
Stated Limits

Where AI shouldn't be trusted blindly — including in what I build.

A trust page that only lists safeguards isn't fully honest. These are the real limits of current AI systems that apply to this work specifically.

Legal AI is a drafting aid, not legal advice

Every AI legal assistant persona in Verity Legal is built and labeled as a tool to assist a licensed professional — never positioned as a replacement for one, in the product or in how it's described here.

Models can be confidently wrong

Retrieval-grounded answers reduce but don't eliminate this. Any AI output feeding a consequential decision is scoped with a human review step — stated during project scoping, not discovered later.

This page is reviewed, not static

As AI providers' terms, regulations (including the EU AI Act), and my own practices evolve, this page is updated to match — not left as a launch-day snapshot.
