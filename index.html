Case Study — Verity Legal

Built solo. Built for production.

The Problem

Law firms manage cases, documents, deadlines, and client communications manually — slow, error-prone, and impossible to scale. No production-grade AI solution existed that understood how law firms actually work from the inside.

The Approach

Building a multi-tenant SaaS platform from the ground up — solo. Architecture decisions made for production from day one: Row-Level Security for tenant data isolation, event-driven background jobs for automated workflows, and a 1024-dimension embedding pipeline for AI-powered legal document search using a law-specific model. Strict test-driven development throughout — hundreds of automated tests at every phase before deployment. Strangler Fig migration architecture across 10 core domains, designed for zero downtime.

Where It Stands

In active development, built in the open. 10 AI legal assistant personas underway — CaseMind, ContractBot, ResearchBot and more — with the core platform already handling real legal document search across a growing law database.
