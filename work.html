Case Study 01 — Flagship Build

Verity Legal — AI legal practice management, built solo.

Multi-tenant AI SaaS for law firms — case management, document automation, AI legal assistant, financial tracking

In Active Development
Before
Manual
Law firms track cases, documents, deadlines, and client communication across spreadsheets, email threads, and paper files — slow, error-prone, and impossible to scale past a handful of active matters.
→
After
200+ laws indexed
A single multi-tenant platform with row-level security, AI-assisted legal search across a growing law database, and automated document and case workflows — architected for production from the first commit.

The Approach

Multi-tenant SaaS built from the ground up, solo. Row-Level Security for tenant data isolation, event-driven background jobs via Inngest for automated workflows, and a 1024-dimension embedding pipeline for AI-powered legal document search using a law-specific model. Strict test-driven development throughout — hundreds of automated tests at every phase before deployment. Strangler Fig migration architecture across 10 core domains, designed for zero downtime.

Where It Stands

In active development, built in the open. 10 AI legal assistant personas underway — CaseMind, ContractBot, ResearchBot and more — with the core platform already handling real legal document search across a growing law database.

Case Study 02

LeadGen AI — automated B2B lead generation.

Replacing manual prospecting with a hands-free pipeline from lead discovery to booked meeting

Backend Live
Before
Manual
Prospecting, qualification, and outreach handled by hand — hours per week spent on research and first-touch messages that mostly go unanswered.
→
After
Zero manual work
An n8n-orchestrated pipeline using OpenAI GPT for qualification and personalization, running the full discovery-to-outreach sequence without a human touching each lead.

The Approach

Workflow orchestration in n8n triggers lead discovery, GPT-based qualification against a defined ICP, and personalized first-touch generation — each stage logged and recoverable so a failure in one step doesn't silently drop a lead.
