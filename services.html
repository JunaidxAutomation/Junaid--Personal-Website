The Process

Discovery → build → launch → handoff.

Four phases, every time, regardless of project size. The scope and duration change; the structure doesn't — because predictability is the thing international clients are actually buying when they hire someone they can't walk over to check on.

01

Discovery & scoping call

A focused conversation on the actual problem, constraints, and what "done" looks like. No sales pitch — if the fit isn't right, I'll say so here, before either side invests more time.

Typical duration: 30–45 minutes
02

Written scope & milestone plan

A document — not a verbal understanding — covering what's being built, in what order, and what each milestone looks like when it's done. Nothing starts until this is agreed in writing.

Delivered before any code is written
03

Build, in visible phases

You see working software every week, not just at the end. For multi-phase builds, each milestone ships something you can actually look at and test — not a progress percentage.

Weekly visibility, not a black box
04

Launch & handoff

Deployment, documentation, and a short bug-fix window at no extra cost — so your team isn't dependent on me for basic changes afterward. The code and infrastructure are yours either way.

7-day bug-fix window included
