Testimonials

Built in the open, not behind a curtain.

A testimonial praising communication style is easy to write and easy to distrust. The bar here is higher: a quote naming a measurable outcome, from a named client wherever permission allows it. Where that doesn't exist yet, this page says so directly instead of filling the space with something softer.

Current Status

Where client proof actually stands today.

Verity Legal doesn't have client testimonials yet — it's genuinely still being built, not parked behind a "coming soon" page. What it does have: a working multi-tenant architecture, 200+ laws indexed, and 10 AI legal assistant personas in progress. When the first real client testimonial exists, it'll replace this card — not sit next to a fabricated one.

Context
